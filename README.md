# Welcome to Songsmith Plugins

**Great sound, without the complicated controls.**

Songsmith Plugins, from Songsmith Studio, is a growing family of audio tools for musicians who record and mix their own music. Whether you're polishing a vocal, shaping a guitar tone or preparing a finished song, the aim is simple: make it easier to get the sound you want.

## Meet the plugins

- **Songsmith EQ** — shape your sound and remove unwanted frequencies, with an optional Frequency Guide that names what you hear.
- **Songsmith Analog Compressor** — smooth out volume changes and add punch and character.
- **Songsmith Maximiser** — control peaks and bring your finished mix up to level.
- **Songsmith Compressor** — even out the level with simple, clear controls and no added character.

Each plugin uses familiar controls, clear explanations and practical starting points. You can begin with a preset and adjust by ear.

## Get started

Download **Songsmith Plugins** from the [Releases page](https://github.com/tonygward/audio-plugins/releases).

- **Windows:** download `Songsmith-Plugins-<version>-win64-setup.exe`.
- **Mac:** download `Songsmith-Plugins-<version>-macOS.pkg`.
- **Only two downloads:** one installer per operating system. Inside either installer, **each plugin has its own tick box** (selected by default). Choose the plugins you want, finish installation, then reopen your recording software.

The plugins work in compatible recording software through VST3 (and Audio Units on Mac). Standalone versions are also available.

**A note about security prompts:** the current installers are not digitally signed. Windows or macOS may warn before opening them. See the [installation and troubleshooting guide](TECHNICAL.md) for details.
