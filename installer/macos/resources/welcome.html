This installs the Songsmith Plugins @SUITE_VERSION@ for every user on this Mac:

  • Songsmith EQ @EQ_VERSION@: shape the tone of a track
  • Songsmith Analog Compressor @FET_VERSION@: even out the level and add analogue warmth
  • Songsmith Maximiser @MAX_VERSION@: make a finished mix louder without clipping
  • Songsmith Compressor @COMP_VERSION@: even out the level with simple, clear controls
  • Songsmith Optical Leveller @OPT_VERSION@: smooth, simple levelling inspired by a classic optical unit
  • Songsmith Passive EQ @PEQ_VERSION@: gentle, broad low and high tone shaping inspired by a classic passive equaliser
  • Songsmith Bus Compressor @BUS_VERSION@: glue a mix or a group of sounds together, inspired by a classic mixing desk bus compressor
  • Songsmith Audio Analyzer @ANA_VERSION@: check the loudness and format of a finished song (an app, not a plugin)

Each plugin comes as a VST3 plugin (for most recording software), an Audio Unit (for Logic Pro and GarageBand) and a standalone app that runs on its own. Everything is selected. The Audio Analyzer is an app in Applications only, with no plugin, and needs no recording software. Click Customize on the Installation Type page to leave out anything you don't want.

Quit your recording software before you continue. Software that's running keeps using the old version until you restart it.

Installed a plugin with its own installer before? This replaces it, so there's still only one copy. Your sessions and presets carry over.
