# Songsmith Studio — roadmap

_Forward-looking ideas, not committed release dates._

## Our principles

**Professional sound. Musical controls. No engineering degree required.**

Keep every product beginner-friendly, consistent in appearance, straightforward to install, and useful for real musicians. Prefer clear language and a few well-chosen starting points over unnecessary complexity. Preserve sound quality, reliability and compatibility.

## Three directions

1. **More plugins** — grow the current EQ, Analog Compressor and Maximiser into a focused collection of approximately **6–12 plugins**, chosen for everyday recording and mixing needs.
2. **Songsmith Channel Strip** — combine our existing plugin processing engines into one easy-to-use plugin, with simple controls and ready-made instrument and vocal chains.
3. **Songsmith Loudness Analyser** — a **separate standalone application** that accepts an entire finished track, measures whole-song loudness (LUFS) and true peaks, and gives simple streaming guidance. Mastering/export could be explored later.

## Next step

Keep refining and testing the existing three plugins with musicians before committing to a build order for the new ideas. This is a direction, not a fixed feature list.
